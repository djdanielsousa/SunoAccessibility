Manual — Accessibility for Suno AI

Version 1.0.1 dev — Daniel SonarCode — DEV

This add-on improves accessibility of the Suno AI website with NVDA.

The add-on announces when creation starts and when the song is ready to download.

Identified controls

Comments, Like, Dislike, Share, home page, menu, billing and creation field.

Diagnostics

In NVDA, open Tools, Suno AI and choose the detailed diagnostic. Options are under Preferences, Settings, Suno AI.

Compatibility

NVDA 2014.1 to 2027.1; Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Brave and Vivaldi.
